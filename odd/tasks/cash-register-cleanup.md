# Cash register: correct breakdowns, real blind close, clean UI

## Objective
Every cash screen adds up to the expected cash, the employee never sees cash amounts (blind close), and the module follows `frontend/CLAUDE.md`.

## Problem (read-only audit, 2026-10-21, spot-checked by the parent)
- Expected cash = opening + cash sales + cash stays + cash in − cash out, but each breakdown omits a component: `cash-register-summary.ts:39` (detail) and `cash-register-list.html` (history) omit `cashStays`; the open-register cash card (`cash-register-session.html:97`) omits cash sales (shown in another card) and shows stays twice.
- Blind close leaks: GUEST does not get `expectedCash` but sees every component (opening, cash sales, cash stays, cash in, cash out).
- Double open: `cash-register-page.ts:66` sets `opening` false before `refresh()` finishes; the form stays enabled and a second tap gets a 409.
- `currentCashRegisterResource` (root singleton) survives logout (a GUEST may see a cached ADMIN summary) and shows stale totals when coming back to Caja; a failed reload replaces the whole page even when previous data is valid.
- Style: `.surface` + borders, off-theme text sizes, hardcoded emerald/neutral-300, `secondary` (white) button variant, raw `btn btn-sm/xs`, `table-sm`, `overflow-x-auto`, opacity text, decorative color on normal rows, amounts without `number:'1.2-2'`.
- Information: ID + pulsing "Turno Activo" noise; history rows expand to repeat what "Ver detalle" shows; difference 0 shows as "S/ 0.00".

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-21)
- Real blind close: the employee (GUEST) sees NO cash amounts on the open register (no opening, sales totals, stays, cash in/out, expected). They see status, who opened and when, the actions (Movimiento / Cerrar) and the movements list.
- Admin sees ONE "Efectivo" breakdown that adds up: Fondo + Ventas en efectivo + Habitaciones en efectivo + Aportes − Retiros = Esperado.
- History: no expandable row; the row opens the detail; difference 0 reads "Cuadrada".
- Use the `frontend-design` skill for information hierarchy/layout only; visual rules come from `frontend/CLAUDE.md` (tokens, no borders, default sizes).
- Frontend only. Backend still sends the components to GUEST (follow-up, not in scope).
- No commits unless the owner asks.
